How APMC Direct Works — Four-Party Coordination

The strength of this wholesale business is precise coordination between four parties. Every order flows through this smart system to ensure correct goods, correct price, correct delivery.

👤

1. Owner / Admin

The wholesale agent at APMC Vashi. Operates the portal, sets the rate chart, manages client onboarding, and orchestrates demand across hotels, fruit shops and restaurants.

🤝

2. Buyer (APMC Yard)

Aggregates orders, negotiates at the yard, picks graded fresh fruits and spices directly from authorised APMC sellers to maintain quality and price.

🚛

3. Transporter

Contracted logistics partner with internal rate card. Loads the truck at APMC Vashi, dispatches via optimal route to your business doorstep across Maharashtra.

🏪

4. Client (Hotel / Fruit Shop)

Your business — hotel kitchen, fruit shop, juice centre, restaurant, sweet mart or caterer. Orders online, receives graded produce at the door, pays online or on delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything hotel owners, fruit shop owners and restaurant buyers ask about APMC Direct.

What is APMC Direct and who is it for?

APMC Direct is an online wholesale portal operated by an authorised APMC Vashi (Navi Mumbai) agent. It is built for B2B buyers — hotels, fruit shops, juice centres, restaurants, sweet marts, caterers and institutional buyers who need bulk fruits and spices at today's mandi rate, with doorstep delivery.

How is the four-party coordination model structured?

The Owner (Admin) sets pricing and client relationships on the website. The Buyer (APMC yard agent) aggregates orders and negotiates inventory at the Vashi yard. The Transporter — operating from an internal rate chart — loads and dispatches the delivery. The Client (hotel, fruit shop or restaurant) places the order online and pays online or on delivery.

Which cities do you deliver to?

Same-day delivery across Navi Mumbai, Mumbai, Thane and Mira-Bhayandar. Next-day delivery to Pune, Nashik and key Maharashtra cities. Custom bulk slots available for hotel chains.

What is the minimum order value?

Minimum basket value ₹2,000 for Mumbai/Navi Mumbai and ₹5,000 for out-of-MMR deliveries. Bulk hotel procurement contracts have dedicated pricing.

Do you supply dry fruits?

Yes. Almonds, cashews, raisins, pistachios, walnuts, figs, dates and kishmish are all sourced from the APMC Masala Market at wholesale rates.

How do I place an order?

Click "Order Online" anywhere on this site, sign in with your business contact in the Apps Script portal, browse the live rate list, add items to cart, confirm the delivery address and pay online. You will receive an SMS/WhatsApp confirmation with transporter dispatch details.
